CAD Design

At its core, CAD design is the process of creating 2D and 3D models of parts, products, or entire systems on your computer. Think of it as digital sketching – but with precision, power, and the ability to simulate real-world behaviors like stress, temperature, and motion. Engineers and designers use CAD tools to go from idea to prototype, to production — all before the first part is even built. Learning CAD gives you the superpower to create, iterate, and innovate at scale.

Author Uma Maheswari K #cad #design
